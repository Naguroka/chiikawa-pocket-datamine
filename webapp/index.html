The formulas this calculator uses (from the datamine)

Per-hit damage

damage = attack × hitRate × attackRate(costume) × bossOrMobRate × defenceDebuff ÷ attackDebuff
crit (one roll < critRate): × critDamage × critDefenceDebuff
expected crit multiplier = 1 + critRate × (critDamage − 1)

Per-slot DPS

normalDPS = attack × normalRate × costumeAttackRate × swingsPerSec
skillDPS  = attack × skillMult(skillLv) × skillCoef × costumeAttackRate × skillRate × uniqueRate ÷ cycleTime
cycleTime = recast × (1 − cooldownReduction) + castTime + 0.5s queue
skillMult = lerp(startValue → endValue over level range) ÷ 1000  (Permil)

Buffs / debuffs (team)

uptime = buffDuration ÷ cycleTime (≤ 1)
attack/crit buffs & defence-debuffs act as ×(1 + value × uptime) team multipliers

Ordering

Single-target hits always strike the leftmost ALIVE slot (lead). Leads die left → right.
Skills enter a FIFO queue in formation order: unique fires 1/0.5s, assist 1/2s.
Buffs must be LEFT of your nuke to affect it. CP number = HP×0.6 + Σ(normal×500 + skill×500) — rough estimate.

Tag synergy uses a configurable weight (default +2%/extra member) — the game scales effects off tag counts but the exact per-tag values are condition-driven; treat it as an estimate. RecastReset effects are noted but not scored.
